# Things To Do & Must-Visit Places in Singapore

## Overview

Singapore offers a diverse range of attractions, from modern landmarks and nature experiences to cultural districts, family-friendly activities, shopping destinations, and dining experiences.

Visitors can combine iconic sights with local neighbourhood exploration to experience different sides of the city.

---

# Iconic Attractions

## Marina Bay Sands

One of Singapore's most recognizable landmarks.

Highlights include:

- Observation Deck
- Shopping destinations
- Dining experiences
- Waterfront views
- Night skyline photography

Recommended for:

- First-time visitors
- Couples
- Photography enthusiasts

---

## Gardens by the Bay

A world-famous nature attraction featuring:

- Supertree Grove
- Flower Dome
- Cloud Forest
- Outdoor gardens

Recommended for:

- Families
- Nature lovers
- Evening sightseeing

---

## Merlion Park

A popular landmark featuring Singapore's national symbol.

Highlights:

- Marina Bay views
- Photography opportunities
- Walking access to nearby attractions

Recommended for:

- First-time visitors

---
