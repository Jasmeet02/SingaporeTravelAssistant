# Singapore Itineraries

## Overview

Singapore offers a variety of itinerary options for first-time visitors, families, culture enthusiasts, food lovers, and travellers seeking a mix of attractions and local experiences.

The following sample itineraries can be adapted based on visitor interests, weather conditions, and travel duration.

---

## Day 1: Iconic Singapore

### Morning

- Marina Bay Sands precinct
- Merlion Park
- Marina Bay waterfront

### Afternoon

- Gardens by the Bay
  - Flower Dome
  - Cloud Forest
  - Supertree Grove

### Evening

# Transportation Guidance

For itinerary planning, visitors may use:

- MRT network
- Public buses
- Ride-hailing services
- Taxis

Most major attractions are connected by Singapore's public transport system.

---

# Itinerary Planning Tips

- Start outdoor activities early to avoid midday heat.
- Keep indoor alternatives available during rainy periods.
- Group nearby attractions together.
- Allocate travel time between districts.
- Use MRT stations as primary navigation points.
