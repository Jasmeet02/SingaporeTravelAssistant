# Singapore Essential Travel Information

## Overview

Singapore is a modern city-state in Southeast Asia known for its efficient public transportation, multicultural society, clean urban environment, and world-class visitor attractions.

This guide provides practical information that visitors should know before travelling to Singapore.

---

## Weather and Climate

Singapore has a tropical climate throughout the year.

Typical conditions include:

- Warm temperatures year-round
- High humidity
- Frequent rainfall
- Occasional thunderstorms

Travellers should carry:

- Light clothing
- Comfortable walking shoes
- Water bottles
- An umbrella or rain jacket

---

## Languages

Singapore has four official languages:

- English
- Mandarin Chinese
- Malay
- Tamil

English is widely spoken and is the primary language used in:

- Transportation
- Businesses
- Hotels
- Tourist attractions
- Government services

---

## Currency

The official currency is:

**Singapore Dollar (SGD)**

Currency exchange services are available at:

- Changi Airport
- Shopping centres
- Licensed money changers
- Banks

Cashless payments are widely accepted across Singapore.

Common payment methods:

- Credit cards
- Debit cards
- Mobile payment systems

## Connectivity

Visitors have several options for internet access:

- Tourist SIM cards
- eSIM services
- Mobile roaming
- Public Wi-Fi hotspots

Many hotels, airports, shopping centres, and attractions provide Wi-Fi access.

---

## Transportation

Singapore has an extensive public transportation network.

### MRT (Mass Rapid Transit)

The MRT system is one of the most convenient ways to travel around Singapore.

Benefits include:

- Fast travel
- Affordable fares
- Easy access to major attractions
- Extensive network coverage

### Public Buses

Public buses complement the MRT network and provide access to areas not directly served by rail.

### Taxis and Ride-Hailing

Visitors can also use:

- Metered taxis
- Grab and other ride-hailing services

## Arrival Information

Most international visitors arrive through:

### Changi Airport

Highlights:

- Multiple passenger terminals
- Shopping and dining facilities
- Airport transportation options
- Efficient immigration processes

Transportation from the airport includes:

- MRT
- Taxis
- Ride-hailing services
- Airport shuttle services

## Safety

Singapore is considered a safe destination for visitors.

General travel recommendations:

- Keep valuables secure
- Follow local regulations
- Use designated pedestrian crossings
- Follow attraction and transportation rules

Emergency services are readily available throughout the city.

Emergency contacts:

- Police: 999
- Ambulance / Fire: 995

## Practical Visitor Tips

Before travelling:

- Check passport validity requirements.
- Review visa requirements if applicable.
- Monitor weather conditions.
- Plan transportation using MRT routes.
- Keep emergency contact numbers accessible.

During your trip:

- Stay hydrated.
- Use public transportation whenever possible.
- Carry a portable charger.
- Keep some SGD available for small purchases.
